Field Screening Scorecard

Run this after the Google Form pre-screen. Fill Part A with the candidate before the role-play, Part B during the role-play, and Part C after a trial day in the field.

Part A

Self-Report

Read each statement aloud if needed. Candidate rates themselves 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). Don't coach the answer.
Part B

Role-Play Rubric

Hand the candidate a real product. Give them 3–5 minutes to pitch it to you as a skeptical customer, including one objection ("too expensive" or "I'm not interested"). Score what you observe, 1–5.
Interviewer notes
Part C

Trial Observation

Shadow the candidate for a half or full trial day on an actual route, then score 1–5 based on what you saw.
Supervisor notes

Weighting: Self-Report 20% · Role-Play 40% · Trial Observation 40%
